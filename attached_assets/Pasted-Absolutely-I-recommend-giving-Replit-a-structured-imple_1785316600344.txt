# REPLIT MASTER PROMPT — KNOWLEDGE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

## Project

**The Science of Professional Textile Care**
Published by **Laundry-Master.com**

## Objective

Transform the existing Laundry Master website from a conventional article/blog platform into a **professional, structured knowledge platform for the global textile care industry**.

The platform must support a three-layer knowledge architecture:

### Layer 1 — 60-Second Knowledge

Short, highly accessible educational content designed for quick consumption.

### Layer 2 — Professional Knowledge

Detailed educational articles for laundry, dry cleaning, textile care, and related professionals.

### Layer 3 — Technical Knowledge

Deep technical resources including research papers, technical articles, case studies, SOPs, best-practice guides, and advanced professional references.

The goal is to allow users to move naturally from:

**Discover → Understand → Go Deeper → Apply Knowledge**

Do not build this as three disconnected systems.

Build a connected knowledge ecosystem where every content item can be linked to related content at different knowledge levels.
