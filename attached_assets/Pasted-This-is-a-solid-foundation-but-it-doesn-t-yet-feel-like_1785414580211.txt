### Final Verdict

The page is **much stronger than a typical WordPress blog**, and the structure is already good. However, it doesn't yet convey the authority of a leading professional knowledge platform.

The biggest opportunity isn't changing the visual style—it's enriching the reading experience. Features like a sticky table of contents, reading progress, article metadata, citation tools, a structured "Continue Learning" journey, richer author profiles, and stronger reference handling will make the platform feel significantly more authoritative without requiring a complete redesign.

If I were prioritizing the next version, I'd implement these in order:

1. Sticky Table of Contents.
2. Reading progress bar.
3. Narrower reading column with improved typography.
4. Enhanced "Continue Learning" section with learning paths.
5. Rich article metadata (reading time, review status, last updated, citations).
6. References and citation tools.
7. More comprehensive related research recommendations.

Those changes would make the site feel like a true professional publication rather than just a polished blog.
